Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who is responsible

This website is operated by Sina Jokar, Antalya, Türkiye. For any privacy question, email cnajokar11@yahoo.com.

What is collected

When you send the project inquiry form, the details you enter are stored: name, email, requested service, optional budget range, project description and your consent. The site language and submission time are recorded too.

Why

Only to reply to your inquiry and discuss your project. Your details are not sold or shared for advertising.

Where it is stored

Inquiries are stored in the website's database and a notification email is sent to Sina Jokar's inbox. Access to stored inquiries is limited to the site owner.

How long

Inquiries are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted, unless they become part of an ongoing project.

Analytics

If analytics is enabled, it only runs after you allow it in the cookie notice. Form contents are never sent to analytics tools.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you sent by emailing cnajokar11@yahoo.com.
