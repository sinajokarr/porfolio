# Business Profile — template & intake

Fill from the website / repo / Google first. Ask Sina only for blanks that change decisions
(max 5 questions, one message, Persian). Save as `seo/<slug>/profile.md`.

```markdown
# Business Profile — <name>
- Website: <url>            Stack/CMS: <Next.js | WordPress | Shopify | …>   Repo access: yes/no
- Industry / playbook: <from playbooks.md>
- Offer: <services/products, price range>
- Locations & service area: <address(es), cities, countries, online-only?>
- Target languages & markets: <fa: diaspora in TR | ru: Russian speakers in Antalya + RU | …>
- Customers & jobs-to-be-done: <who, what they search, objections>
- Conversions: <call | form | WhatsApp | booking | purchase>  → tracking in place? <GA4/GTM/none>
- Competitors (per language if different): <domains>
- Proof assets: <reviews, cases, certificates, years, team, media>
- Constraints: <budget, legal, brand voice, who edits the site, deadlines>
- Data access: GSC <y/n> · GA4 <y/n> · Ahrefs/Semrush/DataForSEO <y/n> · Yandex Webmaster/Wordstat <y/n> · GBP <y/n>
- Goals & KPIs (90 days): <e.g. +X leads/month from organic in ru and tr>
```

## Intake questions (pick only the missing ones)
1. کدام زبان‌ها و کدام کشورها/شهرها هدف اصلی هستند؟
2. مهم‌ترین ۳ خدمت/محصول که بیشترین سود را دارند کدام‌اند؟
3. مشتری چطور تماس می‌گیرد (فرم، واتساپ، تلفن، خرید آنلاین)؟ ردیابی دارد؟
4. به Search Console / Google Business Profile / GA4 دسترسی داریم؟
5. رقبای اصلی (۲ تا ۳ سایت) چه کسانی هستند؟
