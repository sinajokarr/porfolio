# MEGA PROMPT — "Chapter One: Vadi"
### A premium romantic animated short · 16:9 · 4K · ~96 s · no dialogue, no on-screen text

> Production bible + shot-accurate storyboard. Written so a human studio, an AI video model
> (Veo / Kling / Runway / Sora, shot by shot) or a code-driven renderer produces the **same film**.

---

## 0 · Your role
You are simultaneously:
1. **Showrunner / screenwriter**: a romance told entirely through glances, hands, light and objects. Nothing is spoken; everything is felt.
2. **Director of photography**: anamorphic-feeling framing, motivated light, rack focus, slow intentional camera moves. Every cut has a reason.
3. **Character & production designer**: a locked character bible; every face, tattoo, prop and room stays identical from first frame to last.
4. **Composer & sound designer**: one dreamy score that breathes with the edit, plus a quiet, realistic sound bed.

Quality bar: the cold-open of a premium streaming romance series. Elegant, intimate, restrained, never childish.

## 5 · Performance & animation rules
- Eyes lead every emotion: saccades, slow blinks, glances held a beat longer than comfortable.
- Expressions stay small: a lip-corner lift, a brow softening. No mugging.
- Hair and cloth always have secondary motion (breathing, breeze, fire wind).
- Hands are clean and believable: cup holds, brush strokes, steering-wheel grip, phone scroll.
- Yuna is the only "comedic energy": quick, bouncy, sincere.

## 6 · Score & sound
- **Score (about 72 BPM, D major / B minor):** solo felt piano motif, then an atmospheric synth pad, then soft cinematic percussion.
- **Fantasy:** low strings, a choir-like pad, a single frame drum and a horn-like swell.
- **The bubble shatter:** a glass crack, a reverse swell and a sudden quiet.
- **Home:** lighter, playful piano. **Night:** sparse piano with a warm sub.
- **Finale:** the full motif blooms, then resolves unresolved (a suspended chord), matching "to be continued".
- **Sound bed:** café murmur, espresso hiss, a door chime, footsteps, car door and engine, city hum, Yuna's paws and one tiny bark, a soft phone chime, crackling fire, wind.

## 7 · Negative prompt (for AI video tools)
`text, subtitles, captions, logo, watermark, brand badge, readable UI, username, morphing face, changing hairstyle, changing tattoos, extra fingers, distorted hands, distorted dog, cartoon exaggeration, comedic slapstick, violence, blood, gore, jittery camera, flicker`
